Cave Family Bot Policies

Cave Family Bot is a personal automation tool that renders daily episodes of the animated series Cave Family and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Privacy policy Terms of service

Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Who uses this app

Cave Family Bot is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and does not have user accounts.

What data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests the YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload). It uses this permission only to upload videos the bot has rendered, together with their title, description, tags and thumbnail, to the owner's channel.

  • The app does not read, collect or store viewers' personal data, comments or watch history.
  • The app does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google service.
  • The OAuth refresh token is stored encrypted as a secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and is used only by the owner's automated workflow.

Sharing

Data obtained through Google APIs is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Cave Family Bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

The app keeps no Google user data beyond the stored token. The owner can revoke access at any time in the Google Account settings under Security, Third-party apps with account access, which immediately stops all uploads.
